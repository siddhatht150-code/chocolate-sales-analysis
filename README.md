<h1 align="center" > Choclate Sales Analysis</h1>

<h2>Executive Summary:</h2>

This project analyzes sales data to identify trends, patterns, and key performance indicators that impact business growth. The analysis uses data visualization techniques to provide clear insights that support data-driven decision-making.

<h2>Business Problem:</h2>

The business is facing difficulty in understanding sales performance across different regions and customer segments. Due to the lack of proper analysis, opportunities for growth and optimization are being missed.

<h2>Methodology:</h2>

Sales data was collected and cleaned to remove missing and duplicate values. Exploratory data analysis was performed to identify patterns and trends. Interactive dashboards were created using Tableau to visualize key metrics such as revenue, sales by region, and customer behavior.

<h2>Skills:</h2>

Data Cleaning<br>
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)<br>
Data Visualization<br>
Tableau<br>
SQL<br>
Business Analytics

<h2>Results & Business Recommendation:</h2>

The analysis shows that certain regions and product categories generate higher revenue compared to others. It is recommended that the business focus on high-performing regions, improve strategies for underperforming segments, and use data-driven insights to optimize sales and marketing efforts.
